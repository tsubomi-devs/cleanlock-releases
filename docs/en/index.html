What it does

Click the icon in the menu bar and choose “キーボードを掃除” (clean keyboard) or “画面を拭く” (wipe screen). When you are done, hold down the trackpad or mouse button for 3 seconds to unlock.

1Keyboard cleaning

BeforeEvery wipe types letters, fires shortcuts and changes the volume
CleanlockKeyboard, trackpad and mouse locked together

Media keys such as volume and brightness are blocked too. You can lock only the keyboard, or only the trackpad and mouse.

The screen dims a little while locked, so you can see that it is (you can turn this off).

2Screen wiping

BeforeSmudges hide on a bright screen, and every touch sets something off
CleanlockEvery display goes black, and all input is locked

Fingerprints and dust stand out on a black screen. To see streaks, make it white instead. The pointer is hidden too.

Every connected display is covered. Keyboard, trackpad and mouse are all locked, so a hand brushing them does nothing.

3Hold 3 seconds to unlock — never locked out

ElsewhereForget how to unlock, or unlock it by accident
CleanlockUnlocks only when held, and always unlocks after a set time

To unlock, hold down the trackpad or mouse button for 3 seconds (choose 2, 3 or 5). A ring in the middle of the screen shows the progress.

In case you cannot hold a button, it unlocks by itself after a set time (1, 2, 5 or 10 minutes). It also unlocks at once if the Accessibility permission is removed.
